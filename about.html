About Chamcha

A place built around the table.

Chamcha is a restaurant in G-9 Markaz, Islamabad, made for people who enjoy good food and even better company.

Our Approach

Food, hospitality and a room worth sitting in.

Every part of Chamcha is built around one idea: a meal is better when it's shared. From the way the food is prepared to the way the room feels, everything is designed to make people want to linger a little longer.

This is a space for regular lunches, family dinners and everything in between — simple, satisfying food served with genuine hospitality.

The Room

A space made for staying a while.

Warm lighting, comfortable seating and a menu built for sharing — Chamcha is designed to feel less like a stop and more like a destination.
